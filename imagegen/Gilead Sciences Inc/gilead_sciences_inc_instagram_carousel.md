### Carousel summary

- A seven-slide carousel framing Gilead as a clinically powerful but selectively uneven innovator, centered on lenacapavir, oncology, and liver disease.
- The visual direction uses Sunset Boulevard warmth with cinematic dusk gradients, laboratory silhouettes, and disciplined high-contrast editorial typography.

### Slides

Slide 1
Headline: Gilead's New Power Center
Supporting text: HIV prevention just changed, and Gilead is driving it.
Visual suggestion: A glowing injectable vial and orbital halo over a dusk skyline, with the brand name anchored centrally.
Image prompt: Create a premium editorial Instagram slide in a 1:1 composition with a dramatic dusk gradient background in burnt orange, coral, warm sand, and deep blue-green. Show a single luminous medical vial floating above a subtle city-and-lab horizon, with restrained glow and cinematic depth. Center the composition with generous safe margins and large high-contrast headline text reading “Gilead's New Power Center” contained within the middle 70% of the canvas. Use refined serif headline styling, crisp sans-serif support text, no border clutter, and a polished biotech-meets-fashion-magazine look.

Slide 2
Headline: Science Is Accelerating
Supporting text: Lenacapavir publications jumped from 2 to 170 in five years.
Visual suggestion: A clean ascending arc of data points morphing into molecular geometry.
Image prompt: Design a square Instagram slide with an elegant research-data composition: glowing dots rise from lower left to upper right and transition into a molecular capsid-like lattice. Use Sunset Boulevard tones with strong contrast, warm orange highlights, and deep teal shadows. Keep all typography centered and fully inside a safe area with at least 15% padding. Include the headline “Science Is Accelerating” and a short subline about publication growth, using large readable type over a calm darkened overlay.

Slide 3
Headline: Yeztugo Changes The Curve
Supporting text: FDA approved twice-yearly HIV prevention on June 18, 2025.
Visual suggestion: Two precise annual markers and one injection device against a minimalist clinical field.
Image prompt: Create a high-end healthcare editorial slide in 1:1 format showing a sleek injection device and two glowing calendar markers suggesting twice-yearly dosing. Use a sparse, premium composition with strong negative space, high-contrast type, and a warm sunset-to-deep-teal gradient. Headline text “Yeztugo Changes The Curve” should sit centered with generous margins, with the FDA approval date below in smaller contained text. The slide should feel historic, breakthrough-oriented, and mobile-readable.

Slide 4
Headline: Adoption Is Real
Supporting text: 2025 HIV sales reached $20.8B, with Yeztugo launching and Descovy up 31%.
Visual suggestion: A flowing revenue ribbon crossing from clinics to global access nodes.
Image prompt: Build a square slide with a single sweeping ribbon of light connecting simplified clinic, pharmacy, and global map nodes. Use a restrained infographic-editorial hybrid style, avoiding busy dashboards. Sunset Boulevard colors should dominate, with bright coral and sand highlights over a dark teal base. Place large centered headline text inside the safe zone and smaller supporting text below. Maintain clarity, premium polish, and strong text contrast.

Slide 5
Headline: Not Every Bet Wins Equally
Supporting text: Cell therapy sales fell 7% in 2025 despite deep oncology capability.
Visual suggestion: A split atmosphere with one bright lane and one dimmer lane, without turning into a literal chart.
Image prompt: Compose a moody but elegant square editorial image with two parallel visual lanes: one radiant, one muted, representing uneven portfolio momentum. No cheesy business metaphors. Use subtle cellular textures, low-key clinical lighting, and strong centered typography. Headline “Not Every Bet Wins Equally” should appear large and sharply legible, with supporting text beneath it, all contained well away from edges. Use high contrast and a restrained magazine-cover aesthetic.

Slide 6
Headline: Liver Adds A Second Engine
Supporting text: Livdelzi won FDA approval on August 14, 2024 and lifted liver growth in 2025.
Visual suggestion: A warm anatomical abstraction of the liver with concentric therapeutic rings.
Image prompt: Create a polished medical-editorial Instagram slide featuring an abstract anatomical liver form rendered as layered translucent shapes and healing concentric rings. Use Sunset Boulevard warmth, especially burnt orange and sand, with deep teal for contrast. Keep the image minimal, modern, and luxurious rather than clinical-stock. Center all text safely inside the middle of the frame with ample padding. The headline should read “Liver Adds A Second Engine.”

Slide 7
Headline: Verdict: Strong, Not Untouchable
Supporting text: Gilead looks like a strong innovator with translational momentum, not a flawless one.

Use an authoritative editorial finish with Sunset Boulevard tones and strong contrast. Keep the headline “Verdict: Strong, Not Untouchable” large and centered, with concise support text below, all within a strict mobile-safe central area.
