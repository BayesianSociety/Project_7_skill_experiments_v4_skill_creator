# Carousel Summary

The concept frames Demant as a scaled hearing-health innovator whose next proof points are adoption quality and independent outcomes.

The visual direction is Modern Minimalist editorial healthcare photography: white space, charcoal typography, slate accents, high contrast, and restrained product-focused scenes.
